## Release Gates

Season 2 remains a release candidate until controlled evidence covers:

- Fisher and Rancher behavior with nearby players and loaded chunks.
- Citizens navigation through doors and fence gates.
- Blocked-route and fallback delivery behavior without teleport recovery.
- Clean stop, restart, persistence, and temporary-state cleanup.
- Main-thread performance with representative persisted data and active NPC counts.
- No LivingNPC exceptions or repeated watchdog recovery loops during the observation window.

## Operational Safety

- Do not use Paper `/reload`, PlugMan, or hot-loading.
- Do not deploy or restart production without explicit user approval.
- Before an approved deployment, cleanly stop Paper and back up both the live JAR and `plugins/LivingNPC`.
- Do not edit live YAML while Paper can write it.
- Keep Gemini/network dialogue disabled unless separately approved with a non-zero budget and runtime secret.
